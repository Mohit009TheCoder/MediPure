Payment Terms & Conditions

For Doctors on Medipure Platform

Introduction

Welcome to Medipure's payment system. As a registered doctor on our platform, you agree to the following terms and conditions regarding earnings, withdrawals, and platform fees. Please read carefully before requesting any withdrawals.

Earnings Structure

1. Consultation Fees

When a patient books an appointment and completes payment, 100% of the consultation fee is credited to your account immediately. No platform fee is deducted at this stage.

Example

Patient pays ₹500 → Your balance increases by ₹500 (full amount)

2. Platform Fees

Platform fees are deducted only when you request a withdrawal, not when you receive payments. The fee percentage depends on how many withdrawals you've made on that day.

Platform Fee Structure

Withdrawal Number (Per Day) Platform Fee You Receive Status
1st Withdrawal 10% 90% ✓ Normal
2nd Withdrawal 10% 90% ✓ Normal
3rd+ Withdrawal 15% 85% ⚠ Penalty Applied

Important Note

The withdrawal count resets daily at midnight IST. To minimize fees, we recommend consolidating your withdrawals into 1-2 requests per day instead of multiple small withdrawals.

Calculation Examples

Example 1: Single Withdrawal (10% fee)

You completed 4 consultations @ ₹500 each = ₹2,000 balance

Gross Amount: ₹2,000
Platform Fee (10%): ₹200
Net Amount You Receive: ₹1,800

Example 2: Multiple Withdrawals Same Day

You have ₹3,000 balance and make 3 withdrawals:

1st Withdrawal: ₹1,000 - 10% = ₹900 ✓
2nd Withdrawal: ₹1,000 - 10% = ₹900 ✓
3rd Withdrawal: ₹1,000 - 15% = ₹850 ⚠
Total Fees: ₹350
Total You Receive: ₹2,650

You could have saved ₹50 by making just 1 withdrawal of ₹3,000 (10% fee = ₹300)

Withdrawal Process

Step 1: Request Withdrawal

You submit a withdrawal request with your bank details. The system automatically calculates the platform fee based on your withdrawal count for the day.

Step 2: Admin Review

All withdrawal requests are manually reviewed by our admin team. They verify:

  • Your available balance is sufficient
  • Bank account details are correct
  • No suspicious activity

Step 3: Approval

Once approved, the gross amount is deducted from your balance, and the platform fee is calculated. Your withdrawal moves to "Approved" status.

Step 4: Payment Processing

Admin initiates the bank transfer for the net amount (after platform fee). Processing time: 3-5 business days.

Step 5: Completion

Once the bank transfer is confirmed, your withdrawal is marked as "Completed" with a transaction ID.

Important Terms

1. Minimum Withdrawal Amount

The minimum amount you can withdraw is ₹100.

2. Balance Deduction

Your balance is deducted only after admin approval, not when you submit the request. This means you can cancel or modify your request before approval.

3. Penalty Fee Policy

To encourage responsible withdrawal practices and reduce processing overhead, we apply a 15% fee (instead of 10%) on the 3rd and subsequent withdrawals made on the same day. This extra 5% is retained by the platform.

4. Processing Time

Withdrawals are processed within 3-5 business days after admin approval. Weekends and public holidays may extend this timeline.

5. Failed Withdrawals

If a withdrawal fails (e.g., incorrect bank details), the gross amount is automatically refunded to your balance, and you can request again with corrected information.

6. Read-Only Access

As a doctor, you can view your earnings and withdrawal history but cannot approve or process payments yourself. All approvals are handled by admin for security and compliance.

Best Practices

  1. Consolidate Withdrawals: Request larger amounts less frequently to minimize fees (10% vs 15%)
  2. Verify Bank Details: Double-check your account number and IFSC code to avoid failed transfers
  3. Plan Ahead: Account for 3-5 business days processing time when planning withdrawals
  4. Monitor Balance: Keep track of your pending balance before requesting withdrawals
  5. Avoid Multiple Requests: Making 3+ withdrawal requests in one day incurs penalty fees
