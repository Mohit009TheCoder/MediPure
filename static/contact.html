Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I receive a response?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please call our support line.

Can I schedule an appointment through this form?

For appointment scheduling, please log in to your account and use our booking system. This form is for general inquiries and support.

How do I become a doctor on Medipure?

Click "Join Now" and select "Doctor" during registration. You'll need to provide your medical license and credentials for verification.

Is there emergency support available?

For medical emergencies, please call 911 immediately. For platform technical emergencies, our 24/7 support line is available at +1 (800) 123-4567.

How can I update my account information?

Log in to your account and navigate to the Profile section where you can update all your information.
